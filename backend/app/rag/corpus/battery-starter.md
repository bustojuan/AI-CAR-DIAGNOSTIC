# Battery and starter — internal demonstration

Generic internally authored material, not Volkswagen-specific technical evidence.

## Slow cranking

The battery supplies electrical energy and the starter turns the engine. Slow
cranking can involve battery condition, connections, the starter or other resistance.
Sound alone cannot identify a failed part. A qualified technician can assess the
starting system and distinguish possible causes. Do not instruct an inexperienced
user to bridge terminals, bypass protections or work on live high-current wiring.

## Batería y motor de arranque

Si el motor gira despacio al arrancar, la batería y el sistema de arranque son
posibilidades que requieren comprobación. Una observación no confirma qué pieza
debe sustituirse. No manipules cableado eléctrico de alta corriente sin cualificación.
