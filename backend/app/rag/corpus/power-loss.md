# Power loss — internal demonstration

Generic internally authored reference. It is not a confirmed diagnosis or a purchase recommendation.

## Acceleration and warning lights

Reduced engine power with an engine warning indication requires professional
inspection before further driving. Several systems can affect power delivery;
symptoms alone do not establish whether air, fuel, controls or another system is
responsible. Record the operating conditions and available trouble codes without
performing unsafe road tests. Do not recommend replacement parts before verification.

## Pérdida de potencia

La pérdida de potencia junto con un aviso del motor requiere revisión profesional
antes de seguir conduciendo. Registra las condiciones y los códigos disponibles,
sin hacer pruebas peligrosas. No confirma una pieza averiada ni su compatibilidad.
