# Glow plugs — internal demonstration

Generic internally authored reference, not manufacturer documentation or a repair procedure.

## Cold-start assistance

Glow systems provide heat that can assist diesel starting, especially in cold
conditions. A glow-system fault is one possible explanation for difficult cold
starting; battery, starter and fuel-system problems can produce overlapping symptoms.
A dashboard indication or a trouble code alone does not prove that a glow plug needs
replacement. Electrical testing and component identification require qualified work.
Exact plug specifications and compatibility depend on verified vehicle details.

## Calentadores

Los calentadores pueden ayudar al arranque diésel en frío. Un fallo es una hipótesis,
no una confirmación. No se deben inventar voltajes, referencias, pares de apriete ni
compatibilidad cuando faltan el código del motor o documentación verificada.
