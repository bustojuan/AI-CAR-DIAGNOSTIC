# Dashboard warnings — internal demonstration

Generic internally authored information, not a vehicle-specific owner's manual.

## Safety triage

Loss of braking, steering failure, fire, severe fuel leakage and overheating need
immediate conservative advice: stop safely and obtain professional assistance.
Do not continue driving to test the problem. The meaning of a particular dashboard
symbol must be checked against the correct verified manual; do not infer exact
manufacturer instructions from the color or shape alone.

## Avisos de seguridad

Una pérdida de frenada o de dirección requiere detenerse con seguridad y solicitar
asistencia. No sigas conduciendo para hacer pruebas. La documentación genérica no
sustituye las instrucciones verificadas del fabricante para un testigo concreto.
