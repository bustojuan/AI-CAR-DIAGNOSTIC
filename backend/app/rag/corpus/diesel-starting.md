# Diesel starting systems — internal demonstration

Generic internally authored reference. Not a Volkswagen workshop manual, specification,
repair procedure or verified diagnosis. No vehicle-specific applicability is claimed.

## Cold starting

A diesel engine needs adequate cranking speed, suitable fuel delivery and sufficient
heat for combustion. A cold-start difficulty can have several causes. Slow cranking
is a different observation from normal-speed cranking without starting. Ask whether
the problem changes with temperature and whether starting is normal when warm.
These observations do not confirm a failed component.

## Arranque en frío

Un motor diésel necesita velocidad de giro suficiente, combustible y calor para
iniciar la combustión. Pregunta si gira despacio o gira normalmente sin arrancar,
y si el problema cambia con la temperatura. No confirma una avería concreta.
