# Engine cooling — internal demonstration

Generic internally authored material. No vehicle-specific temperatures or procedures.

## Overheating warning

An overheating indication can signal a dangerous condition. Stop safely and do not
continue driving. Arrange professional assistance. Never open a hot cooling system:
pressurized hot fluid can cause injury. A leak or dashboard indication does not
establish which component failed. Component checks require a cooled vehicle and
qualified assessment; this document supplies no repair instructions.

## Sobrecalentamiento

Si hay sobrecalentamiento, detente de forma segura y solicita asistencia. No abras
el circuito de refrigeración caliente. No se puede confirmar una bomba, termostato
u otra pieza averiada únicamente a partir de una descripción.
